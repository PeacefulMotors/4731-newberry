What this used to be

Built in 1906 as a brick four-family in the Lewis & Marshall Addition, City Block 4552. Four stacked flats, kitchens on the alley wall, a parapet hiding a flat roof. Lewis Place private street was laid out in 1890 and listed on the National Register September 15, 1980. The covenant fight here sits in the same current as Shelley v. Kraemer (1948).

For most of a century this was working rental housing. Then it emptied. In 2012 the Land Reutilization Authority took title. From that point it was not a paying four-family. It was a city-owned shell.

What it is now — and what it can be

Boarded shell. Vacant lots left and right. Alley dump pile. Prop NS (NELUX, May 2021) put on a new roof, floor structure, tuckpointing, clean-out, and boards. City investment about $41,825. Later sold from LRA for $5,250.

Why this house is still standing

4734–4736 Newberry Terrace — same block, same four-family type — has a City demolition permit dated September 26, 2007: “HANDWRECK 2 STORY 4 FAMILY BRICK HOUSE,” $5,450. That parcel is now a vacant LRA lot appraised around $4,500 with $0 in improvements.

4731 did not get that wrecking order. LRA took it in 2012 and left the brick up. In 2021 Prop NS spent about $41,825 on a new roof instead of a teardown. It is not standing because someone lived here and maintained it for the last fourteen years. It is standing because the City warehoused the shell and paid to keep rain out.

Who lived here — what the public file actually shows

Deeds record owners, not tenants. The Recorder of Deeds (City Hall Room 126–128; free Fidlar search) will not publish a rent roll.

  • 1906–2011. Four flats. People lived here for decades. Those leases are not on the assessor website.
  • ~2009. Third-party sites list a sale around $19,052 — distress, not healthy income.
  • 2012. LRA takes title. No legal four-rent from here forward.
  • May 2021. Prop NS board-up. Not occupancy.
  • 2023–2025. City list: SOLD — $5,250.

People-search sites listing “13 residents” are aggregator traces, not a verified rent roll. After 2012 this building was not making money. Names on a 2012 deed are almost certainly the transfer into LRA.

Cost basis: $5,250 + ~$268,000 rehab + ~$58,000 power ≈ $331,000. City already sunk $41,825 in the roof. Mid case you are near even with cost.
