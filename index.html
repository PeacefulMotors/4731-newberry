What this building is

Four legal apartments plus a basement leasing office (front, side, and alley doors). The office is not a fifth dwelling. Two vacant lots sit left and right of the building. The roof is flat behind the front parapet. The alley rear already has two stair runs and rear doors — those stay.

Rear stairs and balconies

Do first: rebuild the two existing rear stair runs, rails, and landings. Those doors are egress. A 4-family with no rear doors will fail occupancy.

Real “balconies”: small 4×6 treated-lumber decks at the second-floor rear doors, 42-inch guards, on the rebuilt stairs. That is a landing people already use, made safe.

Side climb-up: do not hang a balcony people climb like a ladder. If you want yard-to-second-floor access on the tree-side lot, that is a permitted stair with footings, landings, and guards — budget about $12,000–$20,000, Gynysis frames it, City permits it. Not Phase 1.
